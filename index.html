What is Caravan Quest?

Caravan Quest is a scavenger hunt through the heritage and nature of the Emirates. Large handmade camels mark places most visitors never see. Your job is to find them.

Made for kids and young people. Anyone can play.See how it works →

The story

Wafi was helping his dad look after the camels while his dad was away on a journey.

In the night, he fell fast asleep and did not hear the camels run off.

Now it is up to you to find them before his father comes home.

The camels

Three kinds of camels

The Originals

Physical sculptures you can find on site. Some take a bit more searching.

The Secretive

Built into something else, or an optical illusion that only appears from the right spot. Some can only be seen in AR.

AR
The Digital Ones

Purely digital. You find them in AR.

Play to learn
Find.Learn.Earn.

Finding a hidden camel scores points. Answer a question about the place correctly and you earn more.

Built with care

Gentle on the land

Each camel is a large handmade wooden sculpture made from recycled materials.

They are meant to be installed with a "Leave No Trace" helical anchoring system, set directly into soil, sand or gravel with no concrete foundation.
