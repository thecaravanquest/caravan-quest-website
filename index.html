Play to learn

Find.Learn.Earn.

Finding a hidden camel scores points. Answer a question about the place correctly and you earn more.

Gentle on the land

Each camel is a stylized sculpture, made from eco-friendly materials and installed with low impact.

They raise awareness of conservation, and they are designed to sit lightly in the places they mark.
